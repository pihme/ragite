Chronicles

The history of ragite as set down so far, newest first.

October 2026

In the middle of the month a humble brother opened a new house for ragite, to bring retrieval into SQLite as a small extension, and first set down a numbered specification. Late that night the first release came, and three more followed by the next morning; with v0.2.0 a model trained for retrieval was built in. Later the brothers wrote a user guide whose examples are tested, and a sister house, ragite-demo, began to search the SQLite books with it.
