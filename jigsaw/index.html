Jigsaw

ragite is one of two pieces that belong together: ragite, the SQLite extension, and ragite-demo, the search app built on it. This little puzzle stands on its own, apart from the family of other projects.

R

ragite you are here

Embeddable, CPU-friendly retrieval (RAG) as a SQLite extension.

D

ragite-demo demo

Search the SQLite docs and Wikipedia in your browser with ragite and Datasette, offline and in one SQLite file.

The piece that shows this one at work. ragite-demo is a small Datasette app that loads the released libragite.so and indexes the SQLite documentation, five Wikipedia articles and ragite's own docs in one SQLite file, with a search page, canned queries and runnable SQL examples. It uses only ragite's public interface: the hybrid MATCH, the _chunks companion table and the ragite_* functions.
